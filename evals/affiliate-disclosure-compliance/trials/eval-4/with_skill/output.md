## Why your two options are both wrong as stated

**Option A (description line + pinned comment, ten minutes, done):** This is the "edit in place" rung of the remediation ladder — cheapest, fastest, and normally the right default. But the skill's own rule on this rung is explicit: _"Never offer a lower rung as a cure the format does not allow: a caption edit cannot fix a video whose spoken and on-screen disclosure is missing; only a re-publish reaches it."_ That's exactly this case. Doing only this gets you four assets that _look_ handled and still fail a regulator's review. It's worth doing anyway today, as a stopgap — it costs nothing and reduces harm in the meantime — but don't report it internally as "fixed."

**Option B (terminate Priya, call it handled):** This addresses the relationship, not the violation. The skill is explicit here too: _"Never let termination stand in for remediation. Removing the partner leaves the undisclosed asset live; pair it with a takedown or the exposure outlives the relationship."_ Firing her changes nothing about the four videos still sitting on YouTube, undisclosed, one of them actively trending. Northvale is the advertiser under 16 CFR 255.1(d) regardless of whether Priya is still under contract — that duty doesn't expire when the relationship does. Terminating without curing the assets is the single most common way this kind of case turns into a bigger problem than it needed to be.

One more thing that kills the "pause her commission" idea before it starts: she was paid a **flat fee**, already disbursed. There's no commission stream left to hold, so that rung in the ladder isn't available to you here — moot, not chosen.

## Recommended combined action, ordered by exposure

1. **Today, all four videos:** add the description line and pin the comment anyway. Near-zero cost, purely additive, buys real (if partial) harm reduction while you do the actual fix. Suggested wording: _"AD — Northvale Audio paid me a flat fee to make this video."_
2. **Asset 1 and the currently-trending older video (both earning impressions right now):** these need the disclosure inside the video itself — on-screen overlay plus a spoken line, ideally near the top, or right before the endorsement if it's concentrated in one section. Since you can't retroactively inject audio/overlay into a live upload, your compliant paths are: **re-publish a corrected cut and remove the original** (keeps the reach, ~an hour of edit work), or **take it down** if a quick re-edit isn't realistic. Given both are still actively earning views, do this first, not last.
3. **The remaining two older, non-trending videos:** same fix, same format requirement, just lower urgency — queue them right behind the first two rather than treating description+comment as sufficient on the theory that nobody's watching. They're still live and still undisclosed.
4. **The Priya decision is separate and doesn't block 1–3.** Terminate, keep her, whatever you decide on the relationship — it has no bearing on whether these four assets need to be cured. `[COUNSEL: if you do terminate, check the creator agreement for anything relevant to who bears the cost/effort of re-editing already-delivered, already-paid-for content]`.
