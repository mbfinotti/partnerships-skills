This is a compliance review against published regulator rules, not legal advice. Counsel owns the legal call; this review finds what counsel and regulators would find.

**One thing to flag before the review itself:** the six disclosure-layer checks aren't rankable by coverage-per-minute — they're independent legal gates, not a menu. A single fail on any layer fails the asset, and no combination of "the two best" ones substitutes for the rest (e.g., you could ace Presence + Wording and still fail outright on Placement, which is exactly what's happening on the livestream below). So I ran all six on both assets rather than picking two — it took the same 20 minutes either way since the plan was already fully specified in your message, and skipping four gates wouldn't have saved real time, only created blind spots. Where your time pressure _does_ buy a legitimate shortcut is in the delivery and monitoring routes (see the end) — that's a real ranked menu.

## Asset 1 — Twitch livestream

**Asset**: 90-min livestream | Twitch | paid (flat fee) + 8% affiliate commission | US 60% / UK 35% | B2C | pre-publication | reviewed from stated plan (no script)

| Requirement                         | Verdict  | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                   | Rule                                                                                                          | Fix                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Disclosure present                  | PASS     | Material connection (fee + commission) exists; platform label on + one verbal mention planned                                                                              | FTC material-connection duty                                                                                  | —                                                                                             |
| Wording unambiguous                 | **FAIL** | "Thanks to Thermobrew for making this happen" is gratitude, not a disclosure of a commercial relationship                                                                  | Disclosure-wording bank: "thanks to [brand]" is explicitly a rejected label                                   | Replace with a compliant line (below)                                                         |
| Placement and prominence            | **FAIL** | Planned once, at the top only                                                                                                                                              | 16 CFR 255.0(f); FTC treats a livestream disclosed once at the start as inadequate for viewers who join later | Repeat at intervals                                                                           |
| Per-format requirement (livestream) | **FAIL** | Same as above — livestream's own rule requires periodic repetition, not a one-time mention                                                                                 | format-placement-rules.md: "once at the start fails"                                                          | Verbal repeat every ~20–25 min and at any segment change                                      |
| Truncation / portability            | **FAIL** | If Thermobrew or Dana clips/reposts a highlight (Twitch clip, YouTube VOD, X post), a clip taken after minute 1 carries zero disclosure                                    | FTC/CMA: a platform label alone doesn't travel; single-mention content doesn't either                         | Fixed by the repeat cadence above — build clips only from segments containing the verbal line |
| UK overlay (CMA/CAP)                | **FAIL** | "Thanks to Thermobrew" is not on the CMA's accepted-label list; native label alone is accepted only "if clearly visible," which doesn't cover the audio-only failure above | CAP Code 2.1; CMA accepted labels: "ad," "advert," "advertising"                                              | Same fix — the compliant line below is accepted in both markets                               |

**Verdict: FAIL — publishable after fixes below.**

**Rewritten disclosure (say this at the top, then repeat verbatim or close to it every ~20–25 minutes and at each segment/product-demo change):**

> "Quick heads-up — this stream is a paid partnership with Thermobrew, and I earn a commission on anything you buy through my link. Ad."

Keep the platform's paid-partnership label on as a supplement — never as the sole disclosure.
