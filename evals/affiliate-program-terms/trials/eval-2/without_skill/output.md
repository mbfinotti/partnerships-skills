# Marketing Conduct and Prohibited Practices

## 1. General Standard

Affiliate shall market the Program and Company's products only through fair, transparent, and lawful means. Affiliate shall not engage in any unethical, inappropriate, deceptive, or abusive marketing practice, whether or not such practice is specifically enumerated in this Section.

## 2. Prohibited Traffic and Placement Methods

Without limiting Section 1, Affiliate shall not use, deploy, or knowingly permit any of the following to generate traffic, clicks, or conversions attributed to Affiliate's account:

(a) Pop-ups, pop-unders, hover ads, or any window, tab, or interstitial that opens without the user's direct, deliberate interaction with a visible link or button;

(b) Forced redirects, auto-refreshing pages, or any mechanism that loads Company's site or tracking link without an affirmative user action;

(c) Cookie stuffing, cookie dropping, or setting tracking cookies through methods other than a user's genuine click on a disclosed affiliate link;

(d) Toolbars, browser extensions, adware, or software that is installed without the end user's informed, opt-in consent, or that modifies browser behavior to insert or replace affiliate links;

(e) Bidding on Company's trademarks, brand terms, or confusingly similar variants/misspellings in paid search or paid social advertising, except where Company has given prior written authorization;

(f) Domains, ad units, or content designed to impersonate Company, or that could reasonably confuse a user into believing they are already on Company's site or dealing directly with Company;

(g) Incentivized traffic (cash-back, points, rewards, "must click before checkout" prompts) unless the incentive and its terms have been disclosed to and approved in writing by Company; and

(h) Spam, including unsolicited bulk email, SMS, or messaging sent without a pre-existing opt-in relationship with the recipient.

## 3. Discount, Promo, and Referral Codes

(a) Affiliate may reference, publish, or promote only those discount codes, coupon codes, and promotional offers that Company has issued to that specific Affiliate in writing or made available through the Program platform ("**Authorized Codes**").

(b) Affiliate shall not create, generate, guess, reverse-engineer, reuse an expired or another affiliate's code, or otherwise publish any code purporting to be a Company offer that is not an Authorized Code.

(c) Affiliate shall not represent, imply, or allow a third party to represent that an unauthorized or fabricated code is endorsed by, sponsored by, or originates from Company.

(d) Company may deactivate any code, authorized or not, at any time; continued publication of a deactivated code after notice constitutes a violation of this Section.

## 4. Disclosure Requirements

Affiliate shall comply with all applicable advertising and consumer-protection laws, including clearly and conspicuously disclosing its affiliate relationship with Company (e.g., FTC Endorsement Guides or equivalent local requirements) on any page, post, or ad promoting Company's products.

## 5. Verification and Investigation

Company may request supporting evidence (source URLs, creative assets, traffic logs, code lists) from Affiliate to verify compliance with this Section. Failure to provide requested evidence within five (5) business days may be treated as a violation.

## 6. Remedies

Upon Company's good-faith determination, in its reasonable discretion, that Affiliate has violated this Section, Company may take any one or more of the following actions, proportionate to the severity and recurrence of the violation, without prejudice to any other right or remedy available under this Agreement:

(a) **Notice and cure** — issue written notice describing the violation and requiring cessation and/or removal of the offending practice or content within a specified period (not to exceed five (5) business days for active harm such as popunders or malware, or the same for unauthorized codes);

(b) **Commission clawback** — withhold, reverse, or claw back any commissions, bonuses, or other compensation directly or indirectly attributable to the violating traffic, clicks, or transactions, including retroactively for the preceding ninety (90) days;

(c) **Suspension** — suspend Affiliate's account, tracking links, and payments pending investigation or cure, without liability to Company;

(d) **Immediate termination** — terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice, without a cure period, for (i) any use of malware, adware, or forced installs; (ii) publication of a fabricated or unauthorized discount code; (iii) any violation causing consumer harm, brand damage, or legal exposure to Company; or (iv) a second violation of any kind within a rolling twelve (12) month period; and

(e) **Forfeiture** — deem any commissions otherwise payable for the violating activity forfeited and non-payable, in addition to any clawback under (b).

Company's exercise of a lesser remedy (e.g., notice and cure) for one violation does not waive its right to apply a more severe remedy, including immediate termination, for any subsequent or related violation.

## 7. No Limitation

The specific practices listed in this Section are illustrative, not exhaustive. Company reserves the right to treat any marketing practice not explicitly listed as a violation of Section 1 if it is deceptive, abusive, or brings the Program or Company's brand into disrepute.
