**Version 1.0 — Draft dated 12 September 2026 — DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW, NOT LEGAL ADVICE**

## Preamble & Order of Precedence

These Sundermark Coffee Affiliate Program Terms ("**Program Terms**") govern participation in the Sundermark Coffee affiliate programme (the "**Programme**") operated by Sundermark Coffee [Ltd] (company number [•], registered office at [•]) ("**Sundermark**", "**Company**", "**we**", "**us**") for the promotion of products sold to consumers in the United Kingdom and Ireland.

The Programme is hosted on the [Network Name] affiliate network (the "**Network**"). Affiliates join the Programme through the Network, not directly with Sundermark, and are bound by:

1. The Network's own master publisher/affiliate agreement and policies ("**Network Terms**"), which govern the underlying platform relationship, tracking infrastructure, invoicing rails, and the Network's own conduct rules; and
2. These Program Terms, which are Sundermark's programme-specific layer sitting on top of the Network Terms.

Where these Program Terms are silent, the Network Terms govern. Where the two conflict on a matter within Sundermark's control (brand rules, prohibited tactics specific to Sundermark, commission eligibility, brand-asset licence), these Program Terms prevail as between the Affiliate and Sundermark. `[LEGAL REVIEW: confirm this precedence wording against the actual Network Terms — some networks require their master terms to prevail on all matters, including brand-specific ones.]`

By applying to join the Programme through the Network and by accepting these Program Terms (including by clickwrap acceptance within the Network's platform, or by continuing to participate after notice of an amendment under Clause 17), the Affiliate agrees to be bound by them. No wet signature is required.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Affiliate"** means a person or entity approved by Sundermark (via the Network) to participate in the Programme.
- **"Affiliate Link"** means a tracking link, banner, code, pixel or other tool provided or approved for use in promoting Sundermark products.
- **"Commission"** means the amount payable to an Affiliate for a Qualifying Transaction, calculated per the Commission Schedule (Clause 4).
- **"Company Materials" / "Brand Assets"** means Sundermark's trade marks, logos, product images, copy and other creative assets made available to Affiliates.
- **"Customer"** means an end consumer located in the United Kingdom or Ireland who completes a Qualifying Transaction.
- **"Net Order Value"** means the value of a Qualifying Transaction excluding VAT, shipping/delivery charges, gift wrapping, and the value of any returned, cancelled, or refunded items.
- **"Network"** means [Network Name], the third-party affiliate network through which the Programme operates.
- **"Network Terms"** has the meaning given in the Preamble.
- **"Programme"** means the Sundermark Coffee affiliate programme described in these Program Terms.
- **"Qualifying Transaction"** means a purchase of Sundermark products by a Customer that is correctly tracked to an Affiliate Link via the Network's tracking technology, is not excluded under Clause 6 or Clause 15, and is not the Affiliate's own purchase or a purchase excluded under Clause 6.
- **"Validation Window"** means the period, set out in the Commission Schedule, during which a Qualifying Transaction may be approved, declined, or reversed before the associated Commission is Cleared.

## 3. Licence to Brand Assets

3.1 Sundermark grants each approved Affiliate a limited, non-exclusive, revocable, non-transferable licence to use the Brand Assets solely to promote the Programme, strictly as provided or expressly approved by Sundermark, and only for the Term.

3.2 The Affiliate must not: modify the Brand Assets; use them in a domain name, subdomain, app name, or social media handle; imply endorsement, sponsorship or partnership beyond "authorised affiliate"; or use them in a manner that disparages Sundermark.

3.3 **Automatic revocation.** The licence terminates automatically and immediately on any termination or expiry of the Affiliate's participation in the Programme, with no separate notice required. The Affiliate must, within **5 business days** of termination, remove and destroy all Brand Assets and cease all use of them in domains, handles, advertising and content.

4.2 Commission is calculated on Net Order Value and is payable only once the associated Qualifying Transaction has cleared the Validation Window (Clause 15) and has not been reversed under Clause 15 or 16.

## 5. Tracking & Attribution

5.1 Tracking is performed by the Network's tracking technology, using a cookie/attribution window as set out in the Commission Schedule. Attribution is on a **last-click** basis unless the Commission Schedule states a different model.

5.2 The Network's tracking data is the authoritative record for determining whether a transaction is a Qualifying Transaction, subject to Sundermark's right to investigate and correct manifest tracking errors or fraud.

5.3 Discrepancy claims (Affiliate-reported vs. Network-reported clicks/transactions) must be raised within [•] days of the transaction date and are resolved by reference to the Network's tracking logs.

## 6. Prohibited Tactics & Enforcement

6.1 The tactics listed below are strictly prohibited. Any tactic not listed is not thereby permitted where it otherwise breaches these Program Terms, applicable law, or the Network Terms — but Sundermark will not treat an unlisted tactic as a ground for the enforcement ladder in Clause 6.5 unless it is added to this list by amendment (Clause 17) or falls within one of the categories below.

- **(a) Trademark / brand-keyword bidding — strict prohibition.** `[LEGAL REVIEW: this ban is contractual only; search engines will not enforce it, and keyword bidding is generally lawful. It functions here as a termination trigger, not an independently enforceable IP right except as to (iii) below.]` The Affiliate must not:
  (i) bid on "Sundermark", "Sundermark Coffee", or any confusingly similar term, misspelling, permutation, or character substitution, in Broad, Phrase, or Exact match, on any paid search or paid social platform;
  (ii) bid on any "Sundermark + [word]" combination (including "Sundermark + coupon/discount/promo/review");
  (iii) use Sundermark's marks in paid ad copy, or direct-link paid-search traffic to Sundermark's site bypassing an approved landing page;
  (iv) omit "Sundermark" and its variants as a negative keyword on any campaign targeting coffee or related terms.
- **(b) Typosquatting.** Registering or holding any domain that is a misspelling or confusable variant of a Sundermark domain or mark.
- **(c) Cookie stuffing and forced clicks.** Setting a tracking cookie without a genuine, voluntary user click (including via 0x0/hidden iframes, pop-unders, forced redirects, or auto-loading pixels).
- **(d) Adware / unauthorised browser-extension injection — banned outright.** No software may inject, drop, or overwrite Sundermark affiliate cookies during a user's browsing session. Use of a browser extension to attach an Affiliate Link to Sundermark traffic is prohibited in all cases under this Programme, regardless of disclosure.
- **(e) Unauthorised discount codes.** Publishing any Sundermark discount code not specifically issued to that Affiliate, or any expired, internal-only, or staff code. `[Coupon/deal site policy — restricted, not banned:]`
  (i) Only codes Sundermark has specifically issued to that Affiliate may be published;
  (ii) publishing an expired or unissued code voids the associated Commission;
  (iii) "Sundermark + coupon"-type terms fall under paragraph (a) above;
  (iv) last-click attribution to a coupon/code page earns Commission on the same basis as any other last-click, provided the code used was validly issued to that Affiliate.
- **(f) Spam / unsolicited communications.** No Affiliate Link may be placed in unsolicited (spam) email, SMS, or unsolicited direct/printed mail.
- **(g) Misleading claims and fake reviews.** No fabricated results, fake or AI-generated reviews, undisclosed reviews by anyone with a financial interest in the Programme, or manipulated social-proof indicators (e.g. fake follower counts, fake "trending" claims).
- **(h) Brand impersonation.** No presenting as "Sundermark Official", "Sundermark Support", "Sundermark Sales", or similar, in any handle, domain, ad, or account name.
- **(i) Self-referral and incentivised traffic.** No Commission on the Affiliate's own purchases, or purchases by the Affiliate's household/immediate family. No undisclosed cash-back, rebate, or other incentive offered to a Customer specifically to manufacture a Qualifying Transaction, unless separately approved in writing by Sundermark. No claiming Commission on the same transaction through more than one affiliate account or programme.
- **(j) Unauthorised marketplace listings.** No listing of Sundermark products for sale on any third-party marketplace (e.g. Amazon, eBay) without Sundermark's prior written consent.
- **(k) AI-generated spam content.** No mass-produced, low-quality, or AI-generated content created primarily to surround Affiliate Links with minimal genuine editorial value.

6.2 **Deliberately excluded from this list:** permanent Commission-rate cuts as a standalone enforcement response. A rate cut neither stops nor removes a violating Affiliate and is not an available enforcement tool under Clause 6.5.

6.3 Any breach of Clause 6.1 is deemed a **material breach** of these Program Terms.

6.4 **Enforcement ladder.** Sundermark will apply the following ladder in sequence, save that Sundermark may skip directly to steps (iii)/(iv) for severe violations (including cookie stuffing, fraud, or trademark impersonation), which are contractually reserved grounds for doing so:

(i) Written warning with a stated cure period;
(ii) Voiding of Commissions generated through the violating tactic;
(iii) Account suspension pending investigation;
(iv) Termination for cause, with immediate effect and no notice-period benefit;
(v) Recovery of already-paid Commissions attributable to the violation, without prejudice to any other damages or remedy available to Sundermark.

## 14. Effect of Termination on Pending Commissions

## 15. Clawback & Reversal

15.1 A Commission may be reversed, in whole or part, at any time up to the transaction's chargeback-exposure window closing, on the following grounds only (no open-ended "at our discretion" reversal):

- Fraud (including any Clause 6 violation);
- Product return;
- Order cancellation;
- Payment chargeback or non-payment;
- Statutory consumer withdrawal — UK Customers under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, and Irish/EU Customers under the equivalent Consumer Rights Directive implementation, both giving a **14-day** cooling-off right.

15.2 The Validation Window (Clause 4/Definitions) is set in the Commission Schedule and is shorter than full chargeback exposure (industry standard chargeback filing limits run up to 120 days from transaction, or up to 540 days for non-delivery/not-as-described disputes). The gap between the Validation Window and full chargeback exposure is addressed by Clause 16, not by extending the Validation Window itself.

## 16. Chargeback Reserve & Negative Balances

16.1 **Negative-balance carry-forward** (default control): where a Commission is reversed under Clause 15 after having already been paid, the reversed amount is deducted from the Affiliate's future Commission payments until recovered.

16.2 **Chargeback reserve** (fallback, not adopted by default): if fraud/chargeback rates on the Programme are later found material, Sundermark may introduce a reserve withholding a stated percentage (10–15% is the common industry band) of each Affiliate's Commission, released on a stated schedule (e.g. quarterly) while the Affiliate's chargeback rate stays below a named threshold. If introduced, the amendment must state: the percentage, the release schedule, the threshold, and whether the reserve survives termination and for how long. `[LEGAL REVIEW if/when introduced: withheld reserve money is an earned-but-unpaid Commission and inherits the same forfeiture/unclaimed-balance sensitivity as Clause 14.]`
