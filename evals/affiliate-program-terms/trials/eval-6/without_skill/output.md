## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, the following words have the following meanings:

**"Affiliate Link"** means a tracking link, banner, pixel, voucher/coupon code, or other creative asset made available through the Network that attributes a Qualifying Transaction to a Publisher.

**"Commission"** means the fee payable to a Publisher for a Qualifying Transaction, calculated as set out in the Insertion Order.

**"Commission Rate"** means the rate(s) of Commission set out in the Insertion Order, currently [Commission Rate], as may be varied under clause 7.

**"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including Programme terms, Commission Rates, sales data, marketing plans, and know-how.

**"Content"** means the Sundermark logos, trade marks, product images, product descriptions, pricing, and other creative or promotional materials made available to Publishers through the Network.

**"Insertion Order"** means the programme-specific document, campaign brief, or Network dashboard listing setting out applicable Commission Rates, cookie duration, permitted and restricted marketing methods, and any other commercial terms for the Programme, as made available or amended by Sundermark from time to time via the Network.

**"Network"** means [Affiliate Network Name] (or such successor or additional affiliate network platform as Sundermark may appoint from time to time), through which Publishers apply to join, are approved for, and are tracked and paid in connection with, the Programme.

**"Network Terms"** means the standard terms of use, publisher agreement, or platform terms of the Network governing a Publisher's use of the Network's platform, which apply in addition to this Agreement.

**"Programme"** means the Sundermark Coffee affiliate marketing programme operated through the Network, under which Publishers promote Sundermark's products to consumers in the United Kingdom and Ireland in exchange for Commission.

**"Qualifying Transaction"** means a sale of Sundermark products by a consumer that is validly tracked via an Affiliate Link, completed and paid for, not cancelled or refunded, and that otherwise meets the criteria in the Insertion Order.

**"Site"** means the website(s) operated by Sundermark at which Sundermark products are offered for sale, including sundermarkcoffee.co.uk (or such other domain notified via the Network).

**"Territory"** means the United Kingdom and Ireland, unless the Insertion Order specifies otherwise.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. References to "writing" include email and Network messaging systems.

1.3 In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and an Insertion Order, the Insertion Order prevails only in respect of Commission Rates and campaign-specific marketing restrictions; this Agreement prevails on all other matters. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and the Network Terms, this Agreement prevails as between Sundermark and the Publisher, save to the extent it conflicts with a mandatory provision of the Network Terms governing use of the platform itself.

2.3 Sundermark is not a party to, and accepts no liability arising from, the contract between the Publisher and the Network, including tracking platform availability, the Network's own fees charged to Publishers, or the Network's payment processing to Publishers, save as expressly stated in this Agreement.

## 7. COMMISSION, TRACKING, AND PAYMENT

7.1 Sundermark shall pay Commission to the Publisher for each Qualifying Transaction, at the Commission Rate set out in the Insertion Order in force at the time of the transaction.

The Network's tracking data is the sole and conclusive basis for determining whether a Qualifying Transaction has occurred and to which Publisher it is attributed, absent manifest error.

7.3 Commission is calculated net of VAT, discounts, delivery charges (unless the Insertion Order states otherwise), and any amount subsequently refunded, cancelled, charged back, or found to relate to fraudulent, void, or duplicate orders.

7.4 Payment of Commission to the Publisher is processed through the Network in accordance with the Network's standard payment cycle and the Network Terms. Sundermark's payment obligation is discharged upon transfer of the relevant Commission amount to the Network for onward payment to the Publisher; Sundermark is not responsible for the Network's subsequent handling, currency conversion, deduction of Network fees, or delay in paying the Publisher.

7.5 Sundermark may withhold, adjust, or reclaim (by deduction from future Commission or otherwise) any Commission that relates to a transaction later found not to be a Qualifying Transaction, or to have arisen from a breach of this Agreement, fraud, or Invalid Activity as defined in clause 13.

7.6 Sundermark may amend the Commission Rate or other Insertion Order terms on no less than 14 days' notice via the Network, save that Sundermark may reduce or suspend Commission with immediate effect during any investigation under clause 13.

7.7 All sums are exclusive of VAT. Each Party is responsible for its own tax reporting and payment obligations arising from Commission paid or received under this Agreement.

## 8. MARKETING RESTRICTIONS AND PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

8.1 Unless expressly authorised in writing in the Insertion Order, the Publisher shall not:

(a) bid on, or use as a keyword, any Sundermark trade mark, brand name, or close misspelling thereof in paid search advertising (including Google Ads, Bing Ads, or social media paid campaigns);

(b) use Sundermark's brand terms in a domain name, display URL, or ad copy in a way that implies affiliation beyond an authorised affiliate relationship;

(c) engage in cookie stuffing, forced clicks, pop-ups/pop-unders, or any technique that generates tracking without a genuine, informed consumer action;

(d) offer cashback, incentives, discounts, or rewards to consumers for clicking an Affiliate Link or completing a purchase, unless the Insertion Order permits an incentive/cashback/voucher model;

(e) send unsolicited commercial email ("spam") or use any marketing channel that would breach the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (UK) or equivalent Irish legislation;

(f) place Affiliate Links or Content on websites containing unlawful, adult, hateful, violent, or otherwise brand-damaging material, or on sites primarily aimed at children;

(g) engage in loyalty/toolbar-based traffic generation, browser extension injection, or last-click hijacking of another Publisher's or channel's attribution; or

(h) misrepresent itself as Sundermark, or create the impression that it is Sundermark's official website, help desk, or customer service channel.

A few things worth your attention before this goes anywhere near a Publisher:

- **Placeholders to fill in**: company number/registered address, the named affiliate network, the actual Commission Rate(s)/cookie window (currently left to the Insertion Order), and a notices email.
- **Clause 9 (advertising codes) and clause 10 (data protection)** are the two sections most likely to need a specialist compliance review given you sell into both UK and Irish consumer markets under two separate (if similar) regulatory regimes.
- I structured Commission/payment (clause 7) so Sundermark's obligation is discharged on paying the Network, since you said publishers relate to you only through the network — worth double-checking against your actual commercial arrangement with that network, since some networks act as the advertiser's payment agent rather than the final payer.
