## 7. Disclosure Obligations

**7.1 Material connection disclosure.** Affiliate must clearly and conspicuously disclose its compensated relationship with Chandlerwick Labs in every Promotion, placed so a reasonable consumer sees it before encountering the affiliate link — not hidden in a footer, an "about" page, a hashtag string, or behind a click-to-expand element. For US-facing Promotions, this disclosure must meet the FTC Endorsement Guides' "clear and conspicuous" standard (16 CFR Part 255). For UK-facing Promotions, Affiliate must additionally use the label "Ad" (or "#ad") — "affiliate," "gifted," "sponsored," and similar terms do not satisfy the ASA/CAP Code on their own. Where a single piece of content reaches both audiences, Affiliate must satisfy both standards concurrently.

**7.2 Written reviews.** Because Affiliate reviews are compensated content, not independent opinion, the following are prohibited regardless of platform:

- Publishing a review of a Chandlerwick Labs product Affiliate has not personally used.
- Omitting or obscuring the material connection disclosure in a review, including on third-party or aggregator sites Affiliate does not control the layout of.
- Fabricated, AI-generated, or purchased reviews, and reviews or ratings manipulation of any kind.
- Suppressing, editing, or incentivizing the removal of negative reviews.

These bans track the FTC Reviews and Testimonials Rule (16 CFR Part 465) and are enforced under the Prohibited Tactics ladder in Section 6, up to termination and commission forfeiture for fraud under Section 15.

**7.3 Company-provided language.** Chandlerwick Labs may publish model disclosure language and placement examples; using them does not substitute for Affiliate's independent obligation to meet the standards in 7.1–7.2 in the actual context Affiliate publishes in.

**7.4 Audit and correction.** Chandlerwick Labs may request evidence of disclosure compliance and require correction or takedown of non-compliant content within a stated cure period, independent of any other enforcement action under Section 6.

**7.5 Health/supplement claims interplay.** `[LEGAL REVIEW: supplement products draw both FTC endorsement/substantiation rules and the FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance — reviews implying atypical or undisclosed-as-atypical results, and any structure/function claims a reviewer makes beyond Chandlerwick Labs' own approved claims, sit outside this clause's scope and need counsel's sign-off on whether an affiliate content-approval step is required before publication.]`
