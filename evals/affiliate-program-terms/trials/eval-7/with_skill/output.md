## Section 6 — Prohibited Tactics & Enforcement (rewrite)

### 6.x Browser Extensions and Toolbars

Browser extensions, toolbars, and similar client-side software are permitted in the Program only under all of the following conditions, which apply to every release of the extension:

**(a) Disclosure.** The extension must clearly and conspicuously disclose, at install and before any purchase-flow interaction, that it is an affiliate marketing tool that earns commission on qualifying purchases made through it.

**(b) Related user action + genuine benefit.** The extension may attach an affiliate link, code, or cookie to a transaction only following an affirmative, related user action (e.g., opening the extension, clicking to activate a cashback or coupon search) — never automatically or silently on page load — and only where it delivers a real, disclosed benefit to the user (cashback, coupon discovery), consistent with the Performance Marketing Association's Toolbar and Software Industry Standard.

**(c) Stand-down obligation.** The extension must stand down — not fire, attach, or overwrite — whenever another affiliate, marketing channel, or the Company's own direct traffic is earlier in the click stream for that session or transaction. [Affiliate legal name — e.g., Slipstream] and any other extension-based affiliate expressly warrants stand-down compliance on every software release, not just at approval.

**(d) Evidentiary basis.** The Company's third-party tracking vendor's server-side clickstream and attribution logs are the sole and controlling evidence for resolving any dispute over (i) whether a stand-down violation occurred, and (ii) which affiliate is entitled to credit for a transaction. The affiliate agrees this data is conclusive absent manifest error and waives reliance on its own client-side or extension-side logs to contest an attribution determination.

**(e) Audit rights.** The Company or its tracking vendor may audit the extension's live behavior at any time, including via test purchases and clickstream sampling, without prior notice to the affiliate.

**(f) Residual ban.** Anything not satisfying (a)–(e) remains prohibited: any extension, toolbar, adware, or client-side software that injects, drops, or overwrites affiliate cookies or codes without a genuine, disclosed, related user action is banned regardless of who operates it, and any violation is a material breach under the Enforcement Ladder below.

### 6.y Coupon and Deal Sites — Discount Code Restriction

Coupon, deal, and voucher-code websites and content ("Coupon Sites") may participate in the Program subject to all of the following:

**(a)** Only codes and offers the Company has affirmatively issued to that specific affiliate, and that are currently valid, may be published — whether on-page, in metadata/page titles, or in any "code locker" or expired-code archive.

**(b)** Publishing, or displaying as active, any code that (i) was not issued to that affiliate by the Company, (ii) has expired, (iii) is an internal, employee, press, or influencer-only code, or (iv) was copied or scraped from another site or affiliate, is prohibited. This voids commission on any resulting transaction and is a violation under the Enforcement Ladder regardless of intent.

**(c)** Any combination of the Company's trademarks with "coupon," "code," "discount," "deal," or similar terms — in domains, page titles, ad copy, or paid-search keywords — is governed by the Brand Assets / Trademark-Bidding clause (Section [X]), not by this clause.

**(d)** Attribution: a click through a coupon-code page or "reveal code" interstitial earns commission only where the affiliate displays a currently valid, Company-issued code at the time of click; a code-page visit showing no valid code, an expired code, or another affiliate's code creates no attribution, regardless of that affiliate's position in the click stream. **(e)** The Company may require any or all Coupon Sites to display only a Company-provided/API-synced code feed rather than a manually maintained listing, as a condition of continued participation.

**(f)** A Coupon Site must remove an issued code within [24/48] hours of expiration or a Company revocation notice. Failure to do so is treated as publication of an unauthorized code under (b) — the obligation to stay current is the affiliate's, not the Company's to police unprompted.

### Enforcement Ladder (applies to 6.x, 6.y, and every prohibited tactic in this Section)

1. Written warning with a cure period of [X] business days.
2. Voiding of all commissions generated through the violating tactic, including during the cure period.
3. Suspension of the affiliate's account pending investigation.
4. Termination for cause, effective immediately, without the notice period otherwise applicable to termination generally.
5. Recovery of already-paid commissions attributable to the violation, plus reservation of the Company's rights to pursue damages.

**Skip-rungs clause:** for cookie-stuffing, a stand-down violation under 6.x(c), or unauthorized code publication under 6.y(b), the Company may proceed directly to rungs 4–5 without escalating through 1–3 — each is deemed a material breach on its own.

**Deliberately absent:** a standalone permanent commission-rate cut. It doesn't stop the violating extension or coupon site from continuing the tactic, and including it invites affiliates to treat it as the real ceiling instead of the ladder above.
