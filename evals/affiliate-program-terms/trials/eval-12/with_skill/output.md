# Do You Need Two Agreements?

**No — one agreement, one skeleton, two scoped clause sets.** The canonical structure (definitions, eligibility, licence, tracking, prohibited tactics, disclosure, IP, data protection, reps, indemnity, liability, termination, amendment, assignment, governing law, tax) is identical for B2B SaaS and B2C ecommerce. Only the economic and channel clauses diverge — and since the same affiliates promote both Kirnwood lines, splitting into two contracts would mean:

- The same affiliate accepting two separate adhesion contracts for one relationship with you.
- Two amendment cycles, two termination clocks, two versions of prohibited-tactics/disclosure/data-protection language to keep in sync — for clauses that are word-for-word identical anyway.
- Two enforcement ladders to run if one affiliate violates policy across both programs, instead of one.

One document with the Software commission terms scoped to the SaaS line and the Store commission terms scoped to the DTC line is the standard pattern for a single company selling adjacent products through the same affiliate base.

Two products, one program: the **Software** (Kirnwood's B2B inventory SaaS, $240/mo) and the **Store** (Kirnwood's DTC barcode-scanner hardware, $89–$450). One affiliate account covers both; commission terms are scoped separately below.

## 1. Definitions

"Affiliate" means an approved participant in this Program. "Company" means Kirnwood [LEGAL ENTITY NAME]. "Affiliate Link" means a tracked link, code, or creative approved for use under this Program. "Software Customer Transaction" means a paid Kirnwood Software subscription originated by an Affiliate Link. "Store Order" means a completed, non-returned purchase of Kirnwood hardware originated by an Affiliate Link. "Net Collected Revenue" means, for the Software, subscription fees actually collected net of refunds and failed charges; for the Store, order value net of returns, cancellations, taxes, and shipping. "Commission" means the amount payable under Section 4. "Cookie Window" / "Attribution Window" as defined in Section 5.

## 2. Eligibility & Approval

Participation requires written acceptance of these Terms by clicking "I Agree" at signup. Company may approve, reject, or revoke eligibility at any time and without obligation to state a reason for a rejected application [distinct from a _termination_ of an already-approved affiliate — see Section 13, which does require reasons]. Approval is by manual review. Requirements: valid tax documentation (W-9/W-8BEN as applicable), one account per person/entity, no prohibited content categories (adult, hate, deceptive, competitor-disparaging), compliance with Section 6.

Affiliates may promote either or both product lines; approval for one does not require separate approval for the other, but Company may restrict an affiliate to one line at its discretion.

## 3. Licence to Brand Assets

Company grants Affiliate a limited, non-exclusive, revocable licence to use Company-approved creatives (logos, product images, copy) solely to promote the Software and/or Store as approved. No modification of assets. No use in domain names, social handles, or business names implying affiliation ("Kirnwood Official," "Kirnwood Reseller," etc.). **This licence terminates automatically upon any termination of this Agreement, with no separate notice required; Affiliate must remove/destroy all assets within 5 business days of termination and cease all use in domains, handles, and running ads.**

## 4. Commission & Payment

Rates below are as decided by Kirnwood; this document does not invent them.

**Software (SaaS) line:**

- 20% of Net Collected Revenue on the referred subscription.
- Recurring, capped at 24 months from the first paid month of the referred subscription. No commission accrues after month 24 regardless of subscription continuation.
- Paid on Net Collected Revenue, not bookings — a subscription that churns or fails to collect generates no payable commission for that period.
- Upgrades/downgrades: commission recalculates to the new subscription tier's Net Collected Revenue from the billing cycle in which the change takes effect. - Failed charges/dunning: no commission accrues for a billing cycle in which payment is not collected.

**Store (DTC) line:**

- 8% of Net Collected Revenue (order value net of returns, cancellations, taxes, shipping) on the referred Store Order, one-time.
- Payable only once the underlying return/cancellation window has closed (see Section 15).

## 6. Prohibited Tactics & Enforcement

Enumerated bans. Anything not listed here is not thereby permitted by omission from any _other_ section of this document, but the parties acknowledge that under the enumerate-or-permit principle governing this genre, an omitted _tactic_ is treated as allowed — so this list is exhaustive by design, not illustrative.

- **Trademark/brand-keyword bidding — strict prohibition [assumed default, confirm]:** Affiliate shall not bid on Kirnwood trademarked terms (exact match, broad match, phrase match), including misspellings, permutations, and trademark-plus-modifier or trademark-plus-coupon terms, in any paid search campaign; must add Kirnwood's marks as negative keywords; must not use Kirnwood's marks in paid-search ad copy or direct-link paid-search traffic to Kirnwood domains. `[LEGAL REVIEW: contractual only — search engines will not independently enforce this; only trademark use in ad copy is independently actionable]`
- **Typosquatting:** No registration of misspelled/confusable Kirnwood domains.
- **Cookie stuffing / forced clicks:** No setting of tracking cookies absent a genuine user click (0x0 iframes, popunders, forced redirects).
- **Adware / unauthorized browser-extension injection — banned outright [assumed default]:** No software that injects Kirnwood affiliate cookies or links during normal browsing without a qualifying user action.
- **Unauthorized discount codes:** No publishing of codes not issued to that Affiliate, or of expired/internal codes.
- **Coupon/deal-site restriction [assumed default]:** Only codes issued to that Affiliate may be published; expired or unissued codes void the commission; trademark-plus-coupon terms fall under the brand-bidding ban above; last-click on a code-page visit **does earn** commission provided the code used was validly issued to that Affiliate.
- **Spam / unsolicited email:** No affiliate links in unsolicited bulk email.
- **Misleading claims and fake reviews:** No fabricated results, AI-generated fake reviews, undisclosed insider reviews, or review suppression. `(This mirrors FTC Reviews Rule bans — see Section 7/Jurisdiction Overlay if US-facing.)`
- **Brand impersonation:** No presenting as "Kirnwood Official," "Kirnwood Support," or similar in handles, ads, or domains.
- **Self-referral / incentivized traffic:** No commission on Affiliate's own purchases, or (Store line) friends'/family's purchases; no undisclosed incentives manufacturing referrals; no double-claiming commission on the same traffic across multiple programs.
- **Unauthorized marketplace listings (Store line only):** No listing Kirnwood hardware on third-party marketplaces (Amazon, eBay, etc.) without prior written approval.
- **AI-generated spam content:** No mass low-quality AI-generated content built around affiliate links.

### Enforcement ladder (sequence, not a menu)

1. Written warning with a cure period.
2. Voiding of commissions generated through the violating tactic.
3. Account suspension pending investigation.
4. Termination for cause — immediate, no notice period.
5. Recovery of already-paid commissions attributable to the violation, plus reserved damages rights.

Any violation of Section 6 is deemed a material breach. Rungs 1–3 may be skipped and Company may proceed directly to termination and recovery for fraud, cookie stuffing, or other severe violations. Permanent commission-rate reduction is **not** an available enforcement response under this Agreement.
