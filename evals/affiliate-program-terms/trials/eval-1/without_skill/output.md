### 1. What the Program is

The Program lets you earn commission by referring new paying customers to Trellisway's logistics workflow automation software using a unique tracking link ("**Affiliate Link**") we issue you. Referrals are tracked, validated, and paid according to the terms below.

### 2. Eligibility

2.1 You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter a contract in your country of residence.

2.3 We may accept or reject any application at our discretion, and may suspend or terminate any Affiliate account that violates this Agreement.

2.4 Current Trellisway employees, contractors, and their immediate family members are not eligible.

### 3. Tracking and attribution

3.1 Referrals are tracked via cookie and/or unique link parameters for **60 days** from the visitor's first click on your Affiliate Link ("**Attribution Window**"), using last-click attribution: whichever eligible affiliate link a customer clicked last, within the window, before signing up, gets the credit.

3.2 A "**Qualifying Referral**" is a new Trellisway customer who (a) was not an existing Trellisway customer or trialist at the time of click, (b) signs up and converts to a paid subscription within the Attribution Window, and (c) is not you, your household, or an entity you own or control (self-referrals don't earn commission).

3.3 We're not responsible for tracking failures caused by the customer's browser settings, ad blockers, cleared cookies, or use of a different device between click and signup.

### 4. Commission

4.1 You earn **25% of the net recurring subscription revenue** Trellisway actually collects from each Qualifying Referral, for the first **18 months** of that customer's subscription, measured from their first paid invoice.

4.2 "Net recurring subscription revenue" excludes taxes/VAT, one-time setup or professional-services fees, refunds, chargebacks, and payment processor fees. Commission adjusts automatically if the customer upgrades, downgrades, or applies a discount.

4.3 Commission stops immediately, for that customer, when: the 18-month period ends, the customer cancels or their account lapses, or the underlying invoice is refunded or charged back.

4.4 We may change commission rates or program terms with 30 days' notice. Changes apply only to new Qualifying Referrals after the change takes effect — commission already accruing on existing referred customers within their 18-month window is not reduced retroactively.

### 5. Validation and payment

5.1 Each commission starts as **Pending**. It becomes **Approved** after a **45-day validation period** from the customer's payment date, during which we confirm the payment cleared, wasn't refunded or disputed, and the referral complies with this Agreement.

5.2 Approved commissions are paid monthly in arrears, provided your Approved balance is at least **$75** (the "**Payout Floor**"). Balances below $75 roll over to the following month.

5.3 Payment is by PayPal or bank transfer (Wise), your choice, in USD or EUR. You're responsible for any fees your bank or payment provider charges to receive funds.

5.4 On termination of this Agreement, your final Approved balance is paid within 60 days regardless of the Payout Floor. Any balance still Pending at termination is forfeited unless it becomes Approved through the normal 45-day process and you request payout within 90 days of termination.

- **German/EU affiliates**: compliance with §5a UWG labeling requirements (e.g., "Werbung" or "Anzeige") and, where applicable, the Medienstaatsvertrag for influencer content.

7.3 Prohibited: spam (unsolicited email/SMS/DMs), cookie-stuffing, incentivized clicks or "free gift for clicking" schemes, misleading claims about pricing or features, bidding on Trellisway's trademarks in paid search, and buying traffic from bot farms or click farms.
