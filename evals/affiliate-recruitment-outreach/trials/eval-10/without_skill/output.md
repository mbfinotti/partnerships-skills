# Outreach Sequence — Touches 2–5

**Cadence** (Touch 1 = Mon, already sent):

| Touch | Day                     | Channel               |
| ----- | ----------------------- | --------------------- |
| 2     | Day 3 (Thu)             | Email                 |
| 3     | Days 6–9 (next Mon–Thu) | LinkedIn connect + DM |
| 4     | Day 13                  | Email                 |
| 5     | Day 18                  | Email → then monthly  |

Touch 3 spans 4 days, not 1: with 90 prospects and a 25/day cap, queue ~22–23/day so the tool doesn't front-load everyone on day 6 and go quiet after. Load it Sunday night so Monday's batch fires on schedule.

Fill in `[bracketed]` placeholders before sending — product name, the specific pain point, and one proof point are the only inputs I didn't have.

---

## Touch 2 — Email (Day 3)

**Angle:** new value, not a re-send. Lead with proof, not the pitch.

**Subject (pick one):**

- `quick one, {{FirstName}}`
- `re: [Pain Point] at {{Company}}`

**Body:**

```
{{FirstName}},

Following up on my note from Monday — figured I'd make it easier to say yes by leading with proof instead of asking for time.

[Similar Company] was dealing with [Pain Point] and used [Product] to [specific outcome, e.g. "cut onboarding time from 3 weeks to 4 days"]. Happy to send the 2-min breakdown if that's relevant to {{Company}}.

Worth a look, or should I close the loop?

[Your Name]
```

---

## Touch 3 — LinkedIn (Days 6–9)

**Step A — Connection request note** (300-char limit, LinkedIn will silently truncate past that):

```
Hi {{FirstName}} — sent you a note by email re: [Pain Point] at {{Company}}. Not selling here, just figured LinkedIn's a better place to trade notes than a cold inbox. Open to connecting?
```

**Step B — DM, sent 1–2 days after they accept** (don't fire it same-day as the accept — reads as scripted, and it is):

```
Thanks for connecting, {{FirstName}}.

Curious how {{Company}} is currently handling [Pain Point] — is it a live priority right now, or more "someday" list?

No pitch, just trying to figure out if this is even worth 15 min of your time.
```

If they reply with anything other than "not interested," move them to your normal booking flow — touches 4/5 are for non-responders only.

---

## Touch 4 — Email (Day 13)

**Angle:** pure bump. Short enough to read on a lock screen.

**Subject:** `bumping this up, {{FirstName}}`

**Body:**

```
{{FirstName}} — know things get buried. Still worth 15 min to see if [Product] fits {{Company}}?

If timing's off, just say so and I'll leave it alone.
```

---

## Touch 5 — Email (Day 18)

**Angle:** breakup / permission-to-close. This is the last touch before you drop to monthly.

**Subject:** `closing the loop`

**Body:**

```
{{FirstName}},

Haven't heard back, so I'll assume [Pain Point] isn't a priority right now — totally fine.

I'll check back in occasionally in case that changes, but I won't keep filling your inbox in the meantime. If something shifts sooner, my inbox is always open.

[Your Name]
```

After this, drop them into the monthly nurture list — pull the send date from touch 5's actual send date per-prospect, not a fixed calendar day, so the monthly cadence doesn't drift as the 90 prospects finish the sequence on different days.
