# ⚠️ Before you send anything — the commission gap is a blocker, not a detail

This skill's rule is explicit: it *communicates* the commission, it never designs or fills in one, and "terms surfacing post-pitch that contradict the email" is listed as a named failure mode. If you send a generic "compelling" email now and drop in whichever number finance picks later, you risk two different things landing in prospects' inboxes with two different economics — which is exactly the bait-and-switch that burns professional affiliates' trust permanently.

So instead of writing one email with a placeholder, here are **two fully ready-to-send variants** — one per option finance is debating. The moment they decide, you send the matching one, unchanged, same day. No rewrite needed either way.

Both assume: B2B SaaS pitch structure, cold first touch, reply-for-info CTA (the safest ask on a cold send — works regardless of what else you can honor today).

---

## Variant A — if finance picks 20% recurring

**Subject:** Hollowpine affiliate program

Hi [First name],

[Specific opener — reference one real, verifiable thing about them: a piece of content they published, a comparable tool they've reviewed/promoted, or how you found them. Never leave this generic.]

Hollowpine is contract management software built for mid-market legal teams — the segment your audience already serves. We're opening our affiliate program and think your readers/subscribers/audience are exactly who'd act on this.

You'd earn 20% recurring commission for [duration — e.g. 12 months / life of account] on every legal team that signs up through you, with a [XX]-day cookie window. [One proof point: customer count, G2/Capterra rating, or a named mid-market legal customer.]

Interested? Reply and I'll send the partner brief.

[Your name]
[Title, Hollowpine]

Both are 78 words in the body (under the 120-word cap for a first touch). Neither leads with commission alone — one audience-fit sentence comes first, per the "affiliates filter by economics, but not economics *only*" rule.

**Fields you must fill per prospect before sending — none of these can go out blank or generic:**
- Opener — one real, specific thing about *that* prospect. A blank or boilerplate opener ("as a legal content site...") is the #1 reason these get deleted.
- Cookie/attribution window — pin this down alongside the commission number; it's part of the same finance decision.
- Proof point — pick one real one (customer count, review-site rating, a named logo you're allowed to use).
- Sender name/title and a physical postal address in the footer (required for US CAN-SPAM regardless of prospect location; treat it as a floor even if some prospects are elsewhere).

## Follow-up sequence (send with the first touch, not after — a first touch alone gets no reply data worth acting on)

| Touch | Timing | Angle | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Day 3-7 | Gentle resurface | "Following up in case this got buried — still happy to send the partner brief if useful." (1-2 sentences, same thread, no restated pitch) |
| 3 | Day 7-14 | New proof point | Short LinkedIn DM or email surfacing a detail withheld from touch 1 (e.g. a customer result, a review-site badge) |
| 4 | Day 14-21 | Easier ask | Offer the lowest-friction next step you can honor — e.g. "Want me to just set you up with a tracking link and you can look later?" |
| 5 (breakup) | Day 21-28 | Loss-aversion close | "Should I close your file? If timing's off now, happy to reopen this down the line — just let me know." Then honor it: no touch 6. |
