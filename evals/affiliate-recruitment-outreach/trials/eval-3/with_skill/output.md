# RECRUITMENT OUTREACH PLAN — Northvale Supply Co., 2026-09-12

**Prospect tiers**
No prospect list was provided — only the email copy. Apply this before your next send: Tier 1 = real traffic + already monetizing (esp. already promoting a competitor — treat that as a _positive_ signal, not a conflict) → deepest personalization, longest cadence. Tier 2 = fit confirmed, traffic/monetization unproven → standard cadence. Tier 3 = plausible fit, thin evidence → standard cadence, small batches, promote on any reply.

**Legal segment**
List is "all US" → CAN-SPAM applies to every send: accurate from/reply-to, non-deceptive subject, physical postal address, working opt-out honored within 10 business days. No prior consent required, but the missing sender/address/opt-out above must be fixed first.

**Chosen angle**
B2C DTC default: lead with one audience-fit sentence, then plain economics (12% per order, ~$21.60 avg. on your $180 AOV, 30-day cookie, monthly payout), one proof point (free returns — reduces the reader's risk in recommending you), one CTA. Rejected: the feature dump (400+ SKUs / app / customer service — none of that answers "why my audience"), and earnings-math bragging (unverifiable, regulatory exposure).

**Tracking plan**
Track contacted → replied → applied → activated in a sheet/CRM, reviewed weekly. Reply rate is vanity; the number that matters is share of approved affiliates who actually produce a click — applications alone told you nothing about the 2 replies you got. Send in batches of ≤50, not 240 at once — small batches measurably reply better (5.8% vs 2.1% in the cited study), and let you catch a bad angle before it burns the whole list. Expect 3-9% first-touch replies as your realistic floor once personalization and the fixes above are in place — 2/240 (0.8%) is below even the generic cold-email floor, which tracks with the template-blast pattern above.
