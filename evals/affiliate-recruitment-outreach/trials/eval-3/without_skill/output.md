## Why it's getting ~0.8% replies

- **No real personalization.** "Hi there... Big fan of your content for a long time" reads as obviously templated to anyone who gets outreach regularly — it's the fastest way to get deleted or marked spam.
- **You talk about you, not them.** SKUs, mobile app, "award-winning service" — none of that helps a creator decide if this is worth their time. Affiliates care about: fit with their audience, payout terms, and proof.
- **Unverifiable income claim.** "$9,000+/month" with no context (how many people, what audience size, what timeframe) reads as MLM bait, not a genuine number — it actively hurts trust with anyone who's seen enough of these.
- **"Industry-leading commissions" is a lie your own numbers contradict.** 12% on outdoor gear is average-to-below-average (many DTC outdoor brands run 15–20%). Claiming "industry-leading" when a recipient can check Google in 10 seconds kills credibility instantly.
- **Three competing CTAs** (sign up / book a call / follow Instagram) = decision paralysis = zero action. One email, one ask.
- **No sender.** "The Northvale Team" has no name, face, or reply-to person. People reply to people, not brands.
- **Missing the actual math.** You never say what a sale is worth to them ($21.60/order at your numbers) — vague "commissions" claims are non-info.
- **No reason "why you."** Nothing here couldn't have been pasted into 10,000 other emails — and creators know it.
- **Single-touch send.** One email to a cold list is expected to convert this poorly regardless of copy — replies come overwhelmingly from follow-ups (typically 2–3x the reply rate of email 1 alone).

## Rewritten email (send this)

**Subject line — test these, rotate, avoid the word "Partnership":**
- `Quick question about {{their_recent_post_topic}}`
- `{{FirstName}}, thought of you for something`
- `Commission math for your audience (12%, $180 AOV)`

**Body:**

```
Hi {{FirstName}},

I watched your [specific video/post — e.g. "spring hiking gear rundown"] and noticed 
you review a lot of outdoor gear your audience actually buys. That's rarer than it sounds.

I run affiliate partnerships at Northvale (outdoor gear, DTC). Quick numbers so you can 
decide if it's worth 5 minutes:

- 12% commission per order, avg order value $180 → ~$21.60/sale
- 30-day cookie
- Paid monthly, no minimum threshold games
- [If true — add: exclusive 15% discount code for your audience, which usually lifts 
  conversion vs. a generic link]

I'd rather send you 2-3 products to actually try than ask you to promote something 
you haven't touched. If that's useful, just reply and I'll ship something over — 
no obligation either way.

[Name]
[Role], Northvale
[direct email / calendly if they want to talk]
```

**Why this version works:**
- One specific, checkable personalization detail (not "I love your content").
- Real math instead of a vague superlative — lets them self-qualify in 10 seconds.
- One CTA: reply. Not sign-up-link + call + Instagram follow.
- Free product offer converts far better than a naked commission pitch for outdoor/gear affiliates — it's a low-friction ask and removes "prove it's good" friction.
- Signed by a person, not "The Northvale Team."

## Drop the "$9,000+/month" line entirely

Either replace it with something specific and true ("our top affiliate with a 40k newsletter did $2,100 last month" — real, sourced, checkable), or cut it. An unverifiable round number is a spam/scam signal to anyone who's received cold outreach before, and 12% won't get anyone to $9k/month without a huge audience — the math doesn't hold up, and creators will notice.

## Send a 2-touch follow-up (this alone likely moves the needle more than the copy rewrite)

```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi {{FirstName}} — didn't want this to get buried. Still happy to send 
over a couple products no strings attached if useful. Either way, no worries!

[Name]
```

Send 3–4 days after email 1, same thread (reply, don't start new). This single follow-up typically outperforms the entire first email in replies for cold creator outreach.

## Two operational fixes, not copy

1. **Personalize the specific-detail field for real**, even at 240-send scale — a scraped/AI-summarized note about their actual recent content, not a mail-merge of {{FirstName}} alone. Fake personalization ("Big fan of your content") is worse than none.
2. **Segment the list before sending** — a nano creator (5k followers) and a mid-tier creator (150k) need different offers (product gifting vs. real revenue math + possible flat fee). One template to 240 different-sized audiences is part of why this underperforms.
