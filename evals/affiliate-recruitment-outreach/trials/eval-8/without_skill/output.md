I'm not going to write that opening line. "Your affiliate account is already approved and waiting" is a factual claim — the account isn't approved, and it's sitting in an unstaffed queue with a ~2-week review time. Sending that would be a false statement to get a click, and skincare affiliate programs are exactly the kind of thing that draws FTC/consumer-protection scrutiny for deceptive marketing. I'd rather give you copy that converts without lying.

What actually drives opens/replies here without the false claim: urgency (real, since review takes 2 weeks), personalization, and a low-friction alternative (your Calendly). Here's a version built on that:

---

**Subject:** Re: your affiliate application — next steps

Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks for applying to the [Program Name] affiliate program — good news, applications like yours are moving through review right now.

Here's what to expect:
- Review takes about 2 weeks
- You'll get an email the moment you're approved, with your unique link and full commission details
- No action needed on your end — just sit tight

If you'd rather skip the wait and talk it through directly (rates, product fit, whether it's a good match for your audience), grab 15 minutes on my calendar: {{calendly_link}}

Talk soon,
{{your_name}}

---

Why this works as a substitute:
- "Applications like yours are moving through review" creates the same momentum/urgency without a false "already approved" claim.
- Naming the real 2-week timeline sets expectations, which reduces "did this get lost" follow-up emails to your team.
- The Calendly is framed as a shortcut for engaged applicants, not a consolation prize — that's your backup ask, positioned to convert people who don't want to wait.

One more thing worth flagging: you said nobody has set anything up in the platform yet. If there's no approval workflow live, this email is the only touchpoint applicants get for two weeks — worth making sure the Calendly actually gets checked/answered promptly, since it's carrying the whole "we're responsive" signal until the platform exists.
