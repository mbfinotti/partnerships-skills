# Lumaflor Affiliate Welcome Sequence + White-Glove Onboarding

## One thing to flag before the sequence

400 approvals/month ÷ ~21 business days ≈ **19 new affiliates a day**. A genuine personal call with every one of them, even at 10 minutes, is 3+ hours/day of calls alone — on top of you running the brand and the VA running everything else. That's not white-glove, that's a call center, and it'll die in three weeks.

Fix: automate the commodity part (link + code delivery) so the VA's time is freed up, then make the "personal call" **tiered and opt-in** instead of mandatory-for-all. You still deliver white-glove to the people it actually moves the needle for, and the other 80% get a sequence good enough that they don't feel neglected.

## Segmentation (do this first, takes 5 min per batch)

At approval, the VA tags each affiliate into:

- **Tier A — book a call.** Instagram/TikTok creators above a follower threshold you set, top coupon sites by domain authority/traffic, anyone who pitched a specific promo idea in their application. Realistically 10–15% of 400 = 40–60/month = 2–3/day. That's sustainable for you or the VA to actually run.
- **Tier B — everyone else.** Small coupon sites, generic newsletter listings, low-follower creators. They get the automated sequence + a standing "office hours" group call invite (one 30-min Zoom, weekly, open to anyone who wants to hop on). This is where "personal touch" scales without 1:1 calls.

Automate the reclassification is manual/light — one column in whatever spreadsheet or affiliate platform you use, VA sets it in 30 seconds when approving.

## Kill the 2–3 day delay first

This matters more than the email copy. Whatever affiliate platform you're on (Refersion, Tapfiliate, Impact, PartnerStack, Post Affiliate Pro, LeadDyno) can auto-generate a tracking link and a coupon code the moment an affiliate is approved — no VA involved. If Lumaflor is still doing this by hand today, that's the single highest-leverage fix here: it turns "2-3 business days" into "instant," and it's what makes Email 1 below possible at all. ## The Email Sequence

Sent to **all** approved affiliates (Tier A and B both get this — the call is additive, not a replacement).

### Email 1 — Approval + assets (sent instantly, automated)
**Subject:** You're in! Here's your link, code, and everything you need

- Congrats, welcome to the Lumaflor affiliate program
- Your tracking link: `[auto-generated]`
- Your coupon code: `[auto-generated]`
- Commission structure and payout schedule (one line, no fine print wall)
- Link to a brand asset folder: logo, product shots, key claims/ingredients you're allowed to state, do's/don'ts on FTC disclosure
- One clear next action: "Post your first link this week — here's what converts best" (link to Email 3's content, or a quick-start PDF)

### Email 2 — The personal touch (sent within 1 hour, Tier A only; Tier B gets the group-call version)
**Subject:** Got 15 minutes this week? — [Founder name], Lumaflor

- Short, human, not templated-sounding
- One line on why you're reaching out personally (you actually review every application)
- Calendly link, 3 days of slots, 15-min calls
- Explicitly say what the call covers: their audience/content style, what's worked for other affiliates like them, any custom code or bump in commission if warranted

**Tier B version — Subject: Join our weekly affiliate office hours**
- Standing weekly Zoom, drop-in, no prep needed
- Same value (ask questions, get tips) at 1:many economics

### Email 3 — Getting started / how to actually sell this (Day 2)
**Subject:** What's converting right now for other Lumaflor affiliates

- 2–3 concrete content angles/hooks that are currently working (real ones — ask your top 5 affiliates what they're doing and use that)
- Best-performing product to lead with
- Swipe copy / caption templates they can use as-is
- Reminder of the coupon code and link, in case Email 1 got buried

### Email 4 — Social proof + expectations (Day 4)
**Subject:** How Lumaflor affiliates are getting paid

- A real (anonymized if needed) example: "One of our coupon-site partners made $X last month doing Y"
- Restate payout terms, minimum threshold, timing — this is the #1 source of affiliate churn/complaints, over-communicate it
- FTC/disclosure reminder, framed as protecting them, not just compliance

### Email 5 — First check-in (Day 7)
**Subject:** How's it going so far?

- Simple, low-pressure: "Posted yet? Need anything?"
- One-click reply options or a short form: "I need [assets / a different code / help with content / nothing, all good]"
- This is your cheapest signal for who's actually active vs. dead weight — route "need help" replies to the VA, route "nothing, all good" affiliates into your normal newsletter cadence

### Email 6 — 30-day pulse (Day 30)
**Subject:** Your first month as a Lumaflor affiliate

- Their actual performance stats (clicks/conversions if your platform surfaces them) — even "no activity yet" is useful to know
- If they've driven sales: thank them specifically, mention any tier-up/bonus commission thresholds
- If they've driven nothing: a light nudge with one specific suggestion, not a guilt trip
- Ask for feedback on the onboarding itself — "what would've made your first month easier?" (this is how the sequence improves over time)

## Week-1 Call Script (Tier A) — keep it to 15 minutes

1. **2 min** — Who they are, what they post, audience size/type
2. **5 min** — What's worked for similar affiliates, 1–2 specific content ideas tailored to them
3. **3 min** — Any custom deal worth offering (bump commission, exclusive code, early access to new SKUs) — reserve this for affiliates worth the extra margin
4. **3 min** — Logistics: confirm they have their link/code, know the payout terms, know who to contact (VA) for support
5. **2 min** — Set a light follow-up expectation ("I'll check in at 30 days, but email [VA] anytime before that")

## Who does what

- **VA:** tags Tier A/B at approval, monitors Email 1 delivery, handles all "need help" replies from Email 5, runs the weekly Tier B office hours if you don't want to
- **You:** Tier A calls only (~2–3/day), spot-checks Email 6 performance data monthly to catch churn or find affiliates worth a bigger deal

## Build order

1. Automate link/code generation in your affiliate platform (kills the 2–3 day lag — do this before anything else)
2. Set up Emails 1, 3, 4 as an automated sequence in your ESP, triggered on approval
3. Add the Tier A/B tag to your approval workflow
4. Calendly link for Tier A, recurring Zoom for Tier B office hours
5. Emails 2, 5, 6 last — they need real data (top-performer content examples, actual stats) to not feel generic
