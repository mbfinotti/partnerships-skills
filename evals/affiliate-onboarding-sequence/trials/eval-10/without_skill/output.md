## Order of operations (19-day window)

**1. Lock the compliance gap — start today, run in parallel with everything else**

- Send every affiliate paid in the last 4 months a mandatory W-9 (or W-8BEN for non-US) + payout-details form before their next payment.
- Hold payouts for anyone who doesn't respond within a set deadline (e.g. 10 days).
- Add the form as a hard gate in the signup flow so no new cohort affiliate can get approved without submitting it first.
- Why first: unpaid tax reporting is a compounding legal/financial liability, not a conversion problem — it doesn't compete for the same time as the email fixes below, so run it in the background starting now.

**2. Fix the welcome email structure — days 1-3**

- Move the tracking link to the first line, above the fold.
- Strip the terms PDF, the 14 banners, and the certification invite out of the welcome email entirely.
- Why second: zero design cost, one edit, and it's the single highest-leverage fix for cohort activation — nothing else matters if affiliates never see the link.

**3. Turn the stripped items into a short automated sequence — days 3-5**

- Email 2 (day 1 post-signup): certification invite alone.
- Email 3 (day 3): terms PDF + link to banner assets (reuse the existing 14, no new design).
- Why here: recovers the content you just removed without re-cluttering the first touch, and it's just re-sequencing existing assets — no new work created.

**4. Build the non-click follow-up sequence — days 5-9**

- Trigger 3 plain-text nudges at day 3, day 7, day 14 for anyone who hasn't clicked the tracking link.
- Reuse existing copy/assets; no design needed.
- Why here: closes the biggest silent leak (dead affiliates who never activate), but it's meaningless until step 2 is fixed — same broken link, more emails.

**5. Segment the sequence by affiliate type — days 9-16**

- Split into 3 tracks off the existing base: coupon sites, cashback sites, Instagram creators.
- Reuse steps 2-4 as the shared skeleton; only rewrite the parts that differ (compliance language for coupon/cashback sites, content/posting guidance for creators).
- Why last: highest effort (copy-only, but 3x the writing), and it improves conversion _quality_ on a base that only works once steps 2-4 are fixed.

**6. QA + buffer — days 16-19**

- Test each of the 3 tracks end-to-end as a fake signup.
- Confirm the W-9 gate actually blocks approval without submission.
- Confirm tracking links resolve correctly in every email variant.

**Rationale for this order:** compliance risk accrues independent of the cohort deadline, so it starts immediately but doesn't block anything. Everything else follows cost/impact: cheapest, highest-impact fix (link position) first, then sequencing fixes that reuse what already exists, then the only genuinely labor-intensive item (segmentation) last, with 3 days of slack before approval.
