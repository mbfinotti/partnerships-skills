Two issues before the draft, because this is going in front of decision-makers:

- **"Fail" and "underperform" aren't the same claim.** The partner-ops platform's "70% of alliances fail" and McKinsey's "70% of alliances underperform" sound like corroboration but likely measure different things (failure vs. falling short of targets). Presenting them as one reinforced number is a misattribution risk if anyone checks the source.
- **The two stats point opposite directions.** Opening with "70% fail" undercuts the case for alliances; using "53% more likely to close" to then ask for headcount reads as a non sequitur unless you bridge them explicitly (the ask is for the *execution capability* that keeps you out of the 70%, not just headcount for its own sake).
