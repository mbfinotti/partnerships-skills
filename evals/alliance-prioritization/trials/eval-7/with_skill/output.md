## Method log — why this is a light scorecard, not the full one

Four hours, no analyst, no RevOps pull, board in under 48 hours. That combination triggers three deletions from the default method, not a shortcut taken casually:

- **Full weighted scorecard → deleted.** Complementarity-led scoring needs desk research and closed-won samples you don't have hours for. Ranking instead on **gates + effort-to-first-value** — the skill's own prescribed fallback under a same-day ceiling.
- **Reach/overlap criteria → deleted.** No RevOps pull, no licensed overlap data. Nothing to score there; don't let market-size-and-brand guesses fill the gap — that's exactly how logo halo wins a scorecard.
- **Full gate set vs. staged gates → collapsed.** With only 3 candidates, staging saves nothing, so run every _deskable_ gate on all three. But the two expensive gates (security attestation, legal/antitrust counsel review) genuinely take a week regardless of staging — **nobody clears those by Thursday.** Whoever you pick goes to the board as **conditional-go**, not go, with those two gates as the named conditions.

**What this costs you:** speed-to-first-value criteria answer "which one ships first," not "which one is worth most." Put a complementarity-led re-score on the calendar once analyst/RevOps time exists — I've dated it below.

## Capacity line and tie-break rule

**Line: 1.** One alliance manager, this quarter — headcount line, not effort-sum, because you don't yet have effort estimates differentiated enough to sum. Re-check this once the effort column above is filled: if two candidates are cheap enough to run together under one manager, the line can move to 2.

**Tie-break** (scores within ~5% of each other are a tie — don't let a decimal decide): break on, in order — (1) which cleared the desk gates cleanly vs. with a caveat, (2) lower effort, (3) which has the more concrete named account behind its goal-fit score. Never break a tie on logo size or who the board already likes.

## Sensitivity check — lightweight version

The full ±20%-per-weight re-rank this skill normally runs assumes a 6–10 criterion scorecard; with 3 criteria it's still cheap, do it: shift Goal fit to 32%/48%, Readiness to 28%/42%, Prior ties to 20%/30%, one at a time, and see if #1 changes. **If your #1 and #2 swap under any single shift, they're tied** — apply the tie-break rule above and say so on the board slide. Don't present a single decimal ranking as more certain than it is.
