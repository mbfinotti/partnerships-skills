# Partner Selection Scorecard — Board Decision, Q3 Sign

**Constraint check first:** with 4 hours, no analyst, and no RevOps overlap pull, a 15-criteria RFP-style model is a trap — it looks rigorous but every cell gets filled with a guess. Below is a 5-criteria model your one alliance manager can actually populate with real signals today, plus the board framing for the gap you can't close by Thursday.

## Scoring model

| Criterion                               | Weight | Why this weight                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pipeline overlap / revenue upside**   | 30%    | The reason to sign at all — but you have no verified number, only proxies (below).                                                                                                              |
| **1-FTE execution feasibility**         | 25%    | You have exactly one alliance manager. A partnership that needs a dedicated integration engineer or a joint GTM team dies in month two. Weight this like a hard constraint, not a nice-to-have. |
| **Product / integration fit**           | 20%    | Does the integration exist, or does someone have to build it?                                                                                                                                   |
| **Commercial terms & exclusivity risk** | 15%    | Rev share, MDF, any exclusivity clause that blocks a better partner later.                                                                                                                      |
| **Time-to-signature**                   | 10%    | "Sign this quarter" is a board commitment — a partner with a 6-week legal review breaks that promise regardless of fit.                                                                         |

Score each 0–5. Weighted total /5.

## 4-hour execution plan (no RevOps, no analyst)

**Hour 1 — overlap proxy, all three in parallel (20 min each)**

- Pull each candidate's public case-study/logo page. Eyeball against your top-50 accounts list (export this yourself from CRM — 5 min, no RevOps needed).
- Check G2/Capterra "Compare" and "Alternatives to [us]" pages — co-mentions there are a free overlap signal.
- Slack your 3–5 most senior AEs one message: _"Heard [Aldwyn/Fennimore/Brightsea] mentioned by a prospect in the last 90 days? Reply with a number, not a story."_ Give them until hour 3.

**Hour 2 — feasibility & fit (20 min each)**

- Read each candidate's partner/API docs page. Score feasibility low if integration requires custom engineering vs. a documented, self-serve API/webhook.
- Note product overlap vs. complementarity from their homepage/pricing page — this is desk research, not a data problem.

**Hour 3 — terms & speed (15 min each + 3 emails)**

- Pull published partner-tier terms (rev share, MDF, exclusivity) — most SaaS partner programs post this.
- Send one email to each candidate's partner contact: _"If we move to sign this quarter, what's your standard MSA turnaround?"_ Even a same-day reply is a data point; no reply by Wednesday AM is itself a signal (score it low).

**Hour 4 — synthesize, score, write the one slide.**

## Decision rule

- Highest weighted total wins the recommendation.
- If two candidates land within 0.3 points of each other, break the tie on **1-FTE feasibility** — it's the constraint most likely to sink execution regardless of paper fit.
- Do not let a high overlap-proxy score alone carry the decision — it's the least verified input you have.

## Board slide (drop-in text)

> **Recommendation: sign [Candidate] this quarter.**
> Scored across pipeline overlap, execution feasibility with current headcount, product fit, commercial terms, and time-to-signature.
> **Caveat:** overlap figures are directional (AE polling + public logos), not a verified RevOps account-match — full overlap analysis to follow within 30 days of signature.
> **Risk mitigation:** structure the agreement with a 90-day joint checkpoint before any MDF or co-marketing commitment, so a weak overlap signal doesn't lock in a bad bet.

That checkpoint clause is the real deliverable here: it lets you tell the board "we're signing with confidence" without pretending you ran an analysis you had no analyst or RevOps hours to run.
