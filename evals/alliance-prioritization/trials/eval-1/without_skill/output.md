# Alliance Shortlist — Recommendation for VP of Sales (Tue meeting)

## Bottom line
Do **not** greenlight all three top-scored candidates as full alliances this year. The math doesn't support it, and the #1 score has a bias problem worth naming out loud in the room.

**Recommended shortlist:**
1. **Piedmont Analytics** — full alliance, lead priority
2. **Solvexa Data** — full alliance, fast second
3. **Northwind Cloud** — downgrade to a lightweight referral/co-marketing motion this year, not a full technical integration. Revisit for full alliance next cycle.

## Why not "just take the top 3 scores"

**Capacity math doesn't close.** Two alliance managers, realistically ~12-14 manager-months/year of capacity left over for *new* integration work once you net out running the alliances you already have. Northwind (7) + Piedmont (6) alone = 13 manager-months — before Solvexa, before any integration slippage (these estimates always slip 20-30%), and before either manager does a single QBR on an existing partner. Picking the top 3 by score commits the team to 17 manager-months against a ~12-14 month budget. Something breaks — probably quality, probably the newest partner.

**The #1 score is compromised.** "Exec relationship" is 20% of a scorecard that put Northwind at 4.8, and the exec relationship in question is the CEO's personal golf relationship with their VP. That's a legitimate asset — but it's also exactly the kind of input that inflates a score independent of whether the partnership will actually produce pipeline. Worth saying to the VP directly: strip that one dimension out and ask whether Northwind still leads.

**Hyperscaler alliances rarely source pipeline for a $38k-ACV vendor.** Northwind's own AMs and co-sell motion are built for deals an order of magnitude larger, or for driving cloud consumption, not for prioritizing a mid-market automation tool in their reps' quota conversations. The realistic value from Northwind this year is marketplace presence and warm intros via the CEO relationship — not a co-sell engine. That's still useful. It's just not a 7-manager-month integration's worth of useful, and it's not what "partner-sourced enterprise pipeline" needs right now.

**Account overlap is the one scorecard dimension that actually predicts your stated goal**, and it's diluted 1-for-5 against brand strength and exec relationship. Worth asking in the room: is Piedmont's overlap score based on an actual CRM/account-list match, or an assumption? If it's real data, that's your strongest signal in the whole sheet.

## The recommended three, by rationale

**Piedmont Analytics (lead)** — Second-highest score, and unlike Northwind its case doesn't rest on a personal relationship. Analytics + workflow automation is a natural enterprise bundle, which is the kind of joint story that turns into sourced pipeline rather than a logo on a slide. 6 manager-months is a real but bounded cost.

**Solvexa Data (second)** — Lowest integration cost of the three (4 manager-months) despite a lower composite score (3.2). Cheapest way to prove the alliance motion works and get a second partner live in the same year without maxing out both managers. If Piedmont slips, Solvexa is the one that still ships on time.

**Northwind Cloud (downgrade, don't drop)** — Keep the relationship warm — it cost nothing to originate and the CEO tie is real capital — but don't spend 7 manager-months of scarce integration bandwidth on it until there's evidence (not a golf relationship) that it converts to enterprise pipeline. Ask Northwind for a lightweight co-marketing or marketplace listing this year; revisit full integration next cycle once Piedmont/Solvexa prove out the operating model.

## Open question to flag, not resolve, in the room
The other six candidates (2-5 manager-months each, scores not shared here) aren't in this shortlist because their scorecard values weren't in front of me. If any of them scores close to Solvexa's 3.2 with a 2-3 manager-month integration, it may be a better use of the leftover capacity than the Northwind lightweight motion. Worth a five-minute gut check with the VP before the list is final.

## What this buys you
Piedmont (6) + Solvexa (4) = 10 manager-months against ~12-14 available — leaves real buffer for slippage or for the lightweight Northwind motion, instead of committing both managers past capacity on day one.
