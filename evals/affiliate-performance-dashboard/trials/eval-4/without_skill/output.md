## Board Deck — Partner Program Slide

**One flag before the numbers: verify no overlap between "sourced" and "influenced" in the CRM before summing them.** If your CRM's influence tag can apply to a deal that's _also_ tagged sourced, $5.5M double-counts and a board member doing math in their head will catch it. If the two tags are mutually exclusive by definition (sourced = partner-originated first touch; influenced = partner touched a deal your team originated, no first-touch credit), the sum is legitimate. This is a two-minute CRM query — check it before Thursday. Everything below assumes the sum is clean.

- One hero number, nothing else competing for it — 48px+, plain sans (no display/serif), proportional (not tabular) figures.
- The $2.1M / $3.4M split sits directly under the headline in smaller, muted-ink text — not hidden in a footnote. Showing the composition _next to_ the big number is what makes $5.5M defensible instead of suspicious. A board that can see how a number was built stops asking how it was built.
- Swap in your actual total-ARR figure for the "% of total ARR" bar — I don't have it, so this is a placeholder. That percentage is arguably more useful to the board than the raw dollar figure.

### Supporting row (4 tiles beneath the hero)

| Tile                     | Shows                   | Why it belongs here                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **% of total ARR**       | e.g. 38%                | Turns $5.5M from a vanity number into a statement about channel dependency — boards read this as "how exposed are we to partners."                                                                   |
| **YoY growth**           | e.g. +42%               | A flat or shrinking partner-attributed ARR next to a big absolute number is a bigger board question than the number itself. Growth rate is what justifies continued investment in the program.       |
| **Active partners**      | e.g. 61 of 250          | With 250 mostly-agency partners, the real story is activation rate, not headcount. 250 partners producing $5.5M vs. 61 producing it says very different things about program health and scalability. |
| **Top-10 concentration** | e.g. 54% of partner ARR | Agency/consultancy partner bases concentrate hard. If 10 firms drive most of the number, that's a key-relationship risk the board should see now, not discover later.                                |

Layout: hero tile full-width (or 2/3 width) up top, the four supporting tiles in a single row beneath at roughly half the hero's visual weight — same type family, no competing hero-sized numbers (exactly one hero figure per view is the rule; everything else is a stat tile, visually subordinate).
