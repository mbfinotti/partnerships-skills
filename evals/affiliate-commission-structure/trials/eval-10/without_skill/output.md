**30% recurring commission, capped at the first 12 months.**

That's the right number to put on the landing page today. Reasoning, short:

- 20–30% is the real market range, not just something you're imagining — it's genuinely the most common commission shape used across live B2B SaaS partner programs right now.
- 30% is the single most common exact figure among those, more common than 25% or 20% individually. Landing on it isn't cautious guessing, it's matching what affiliates already expect and compare offers against.
- 12-month cap, not lifetime. Lifetime recurring is a bigger commitment than you can properly cost out in three weeks, and a capped year gives you room to renegotiate once you have real payback-period data.
- Skip tiers, loyalty bumps, or CPA-vs-revshare menus for launch. One flat number is faster to build, faster to explain to affiliates, and faster for you to defend later if you need to change it.

One thing to sanity-check before it goes live, not before you ship the page: 30% of monthly recurring revenue only works if your gross margin comfortably clears that plus your CAC elsewhere. If your gross margin is under ~70%, drop to 20–25% instead — same structure, just don't let the commission eat the margin. If margin's fine, ship 30%/12 months and move on.
