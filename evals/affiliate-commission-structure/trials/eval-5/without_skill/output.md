## Formwork Analytics — Bronze/Silver/Gold commission ladder

**Metric:** cumulative active paying customers currently referred (net of churn), recalculated monthly. Ladder only moves an affiliate up automatically; a downgrade only triggers after 2 consecutive months below the lower breakpoint, so a single churned referral doesn't yank someone back down mid-recruiting-pitch.

| Tier      | Recurring commission | Breakpoint (active referred customers) |
| --------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 🥉 Bronze | 20%                  | 0–4 (entry tier, day one)              |
| 🥈 Silver | 25%                  | 5–14                                   |
| 🥇 Gold   | 30%                  | 15+                                    |

**Why these breakpoints:**

- 5 and 15 are reachable in a first 6-month push, which is what a recruiting pitch needs — an aspirational tier nobody can plausibly hit reads as fake.
- Gap between tiers (5, then 10 more) keeps the climb feel continuous rather than a wall right after entry.
- 20/25/30 are 5-point steps — easy to state on a call, easy for an affiliate to compute their own upside.

**Two things this ladder doesn't fix yet, worth deciding before the six-week launch:**

- Recurring commission cap (e.g. 12 months vs. lifetime) and payout hold period — not part of the rate/breakpoint ask but they change the real value of "30%."
- Whether tier is evaluated on _active_ referrals only (recommended, ties commission to retained revenue) or lifetime cumulative signups (easier to track at zero-affiliate launch, but rewards volume over retention).
