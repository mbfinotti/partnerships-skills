## 1. Internal decision memo

**Subject: Affiliate commission rate change — 20% → 35% recurring, 12-month cap**

**Decision:** Raise the Sablefin affiliate program's recurring commission from 20% to 35% of subscription revenue, capped at 12 months per referred customer. Effective date: [set date].

**Why now:** Affiliate-sourced revenue has been flat for three consecutive quarters. Public competitor programs advertise 30% recurring; a 35% headline positions Sablefin as the top-paying option in the category.

**Unit economics at the new rate:**

- Plan price: $199/mo, 79% gross margin → $157.21 gross profit/mo per customer.
- Commission at 35%, 12-month cap: $69.65/mo × 12 = $835.80 total, undiscounted.
- Against a $3,144 lifetime gross profit per customer (20-month average tenure), this commission consumes 26.6% of LTV.
- Against the $740 blended CAC, affiliate-sourced CAC at full 35%/12mo payout runs **above** the company-wide average CAC, before platform and program-ops overhead.

**Risk flagged for the record:** the program's core problem — 11 of 412 approved affiliates producing any conversion last quarter — is an activation problem, not a price problem. This rate change does not address it and increases per-customer cost on the (small) segment of affiliates already converting, without a mechanism to recruit or activate new producers. Recommend pairing this change with an affiliate reactivation push (audit the 401 non-producers, prune inactive accounts, re-onboard the rest) so the higher rate isn't just a richer payout to the same 11 people.

**Financial exposure:** if affiliate volume stays flat, the rate change costs (35%−20%) × $199 × 12 = $358.20 in extra commission per existing converting customer per year, borne only by the current ~11 active affiliates' referrals — bounded, low-risk test of whether rate was ever the constraint.

**Recommendation:** Approve as a 90-day test. Track weekly: new affiliate sign-ups, first-conversion rate among the 401 currently-dormant affiliates, and CAC by channel. If the dormant-affiliate conversion rate hasn't moved by day 60, the rate wasn't the lever — pull it back to 30% and redirect budget to recruitment/enablement.
